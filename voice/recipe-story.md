# Recipe stories

### Short, and about where the recipe came from

A paragraph or two: where I was, who it was for, why I started making it. The ssamjang wings start on a backpacking trail.

Why: the story is the reason it's my recipe and not just a recipe, but nobody wants to scroll past an essay to get to the food.
When unsure: if it's longer than the recipe table, trim it.

### First person, past tense

It's a story, so it reads like one ("I was backpacking with a friend...").

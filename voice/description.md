# Descriptions

### One or two sentences, in my voice

Say what the post is about and why someone would read it, as I'd say it ("Being new lets you see what a team has stopped seeing. Context is what helps the team hear you say it.").

Why: it's the first thing a reader sees on the homepage, so it should sound like the post, not like a meta tag.
When unsure: under about 200 characters, and no "In this post, I...".

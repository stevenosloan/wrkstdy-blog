# Photos: alt text and captions

### Alt text describes, captions tell

Alt text says what's in the photo for someone who can't see it ("Seasoned wings on a wire rack over a sheet pan, with a small fan pointed at them"). The caption, on the line right under the image, adds what the photo doesn't show ("Salted, peppered, and drying under a fan while the oven preheats.").

Why: they serve different readers. Repeating the alt text as a caption wastes both.

### Captions are short sentences

One line, sentence case, ending with a period.

Why: matches the existing posts, and keeps captions from turning into paragraphs.
